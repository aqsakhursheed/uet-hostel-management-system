How to Apply

Get your hostel seat in three easy steps

1

Fill the Form

Visit the Student Portal and fill in your personal, academic, and room preference details. Takes less than 5 minutes.

2

Get Your ID

On submission you receive a unique Application ID. Use it anytime to check your real-time application status.

3

Move In

Once approved by the hostel admin, your room is assigned. Report to the hostel office with documents and settle in.
